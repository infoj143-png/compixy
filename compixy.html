About Compixy Image Toolkit

Compixy is a free, browser-based image toolkit that lets you resize, compress, and convert images between PNG, JPG, and WebP formats without installing software or creating an account. Every operation — reading the file, resizing the canvas, and re-encoding the output — happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photos are never uploaded to a server, which makes Compixy a private option for resizing personal or sensitive images.

Common uses include preparing app icons (512×512, 256×256, 128×128) for Android or iOS store listings, resizing product photos for e-commerce listings, shrinking images for faster website loading, converting PNG screenshots to smaller WebP files, and compressing photos before sending them over WhatsApp or email.

How to Resize or Convert an Image

  1. Upload your PNG, JPG, or WebP image by dragging it in or browsing your files.
  2. Enter a custom width and height, or choose a quick preset like 512×512 for app icons.
  3. Optionally choose a new format (PNG, JPG, or WebP) and adjust the compression quality.
  4. Click "Convert & Resize Image" and download the result — no upload, no signup, no watermark.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image online for free?

Upload your image to Compixy, enter the width and height you need (or pick a preset like 512×512 for app icons), and click Convert & Resize. The resized image downloads directly from your browser with no upload to a server.

Does resizing an image reduce its quality?

Compixy uses high-quality smoothing when resizing, which minimizes quality loss. You can also control the compression quality slider to balance file size against visual quality.

Can I convert PNG to WebP or JPG online?

Yes. Compixy lets you convert between PNG, JPG, and WebP formats directly in your browser. Choose your target format from the dropdown before processing.

Is Compixy safe to use for private photos?

Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded to a server, so they stay completely private.

What image size do I need for an app icon?

Common app icon sizes are 512×512 for Android Play Store listings and 256×256 or 128×128 for smaller icon assets. Compixy includes one-click presets for these sizes.
