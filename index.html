About Compixy Image Toolkit

Compixy is a streamlined, browser-based web utility designed for developers, content creators, and digital marketers. Whether you need to resize website banners, compress PNG icons for app stores, or reduce JPEG file weights to improve site performance, Compixy executes all tasks locally on your device for maximum privacy and speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are my uploaded images safe?

A: Yes! Compixy processes everything inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never uploaded or stored on any external server.

Q: What image formats are supported?

A: You can upload standard web formats including PNG, JPEG, JPG, and WebP.
